About MovieKnowledge

MovieKnowledge is a collection of games for people who love movies. Play the Daily Challenge, test your film knowledge, or explore games inspired by Letterboxd and its community.

Independent Project

Made by a film fan for other film fans. Some games take inspiration from Letterboxd, but MovieKnowledge is not affiliated with or endorsed by Letterboxd.

How to Play

Pick a game from the homepage and follow its instructions. Identify scenes and quotes, connect casts and directors, compare movie ratings, or try a Letterboxd-themed game.

Future Plans

More movie games and improvements are on the way. Have an idea for a game? Send it through the contact link below.
